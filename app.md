## Feature summary (for site copy)

1. **Extract Audio** — pick a video, tap *Extract Audio*, get MP3 or M4A.
2. **Trim Video Sound** — drag handles on the timeline to keep only the part you need.
3. **Share Extension** — from Photos or Files tap Share → Extract Audio (no need to open the app first).
4. **Save / Share** — save to Files, AirDrop, or send to any app via the iOS share sheet.
5. **Library** — all extracted files listed in-app with duration, size, date.
6. **On-device** — conversion runs on the iPhone; no upload, no account for basic use.
