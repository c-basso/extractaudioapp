How it works

How to extract audio from video on iPhone

Four taps from video to MP3 or M4A. Start in the app or from the Share button in Photos.

  1. 1

    Pick a video

    Open the app and choose a video from Photos or Files — or tap Share on any video and choose Extract Audio.

  2. 2

    Trim if you need to

    Tap Trim Video and drag the yellow handles to keep only the part you want. Optional.

  3. 3

    Tap Extract Audio

    The soundtrack is converted to MP3 or M4A on your iPhone in seconds.

  4. 4

    Save or share

    Save the audio to Files, AirDrop it, or send it to any app. Every file stays in your library.

FAQ

Video-to-audio questions, answered

How do I extract audio from a video on iPhone?

Open Extract Audio from Video⁺, pick the video, trim if needed and tap Extract Audio. The soundtrack is saved as MP3 or M4A on your iPhone. You can also start from the Share button in Photos.

Learn more →

How do I convert a video to MP3 on iPhone?

Share the video to Extract Audio, tap Extract Audio and choose MP3. The MP3 is saved on your device and can be sent to Files, AirDrop or any app.

Learn more →

Can I convert MP4 to MP3 on iPhone for free?

Yes. Open the MP4 from Files or Photos, share it to Extract Audio and export as MP3. Core conversion is free.

Learn more →

How do I turn an iPhone MOV video into audio?

iPhone camera videos are MOV files. Share the clip from Photos to Extract Audio and export it as MP3 or M4A.

Learn more →

Can I save audio from a video as M4A?

Yes. Choose M4A when you extract. M4A is Apple’s native format — smaller than MP3 and ideal for ringtones and GarageBand.

Learn more →

Can I extract audio from video on iPhone without an app?

Yes, with a Shortcut that uses Encode Media with Audio Only. It exports M4A only and cannot trim, so an app is faster for MP3 or short clips.

Learn more →

Is it safe to extract audio from video online?

Online tools require uploading your video to a server. Extract Audio from Video⁺ converts on your iPhone, so your videos never leave the device.

Learn more →

How do I save a song from a video on my iPhone?

Share the video to Extract Audio, trim around the song and tap Extract Audio. Save music only from videos you own or have rights to.

Learn more →

Can I extract only part of the audio from a video?

Yes. Tap Trim Video, drag the handles around the part you need and extract. Only that section is saved.

Learn more →

How do I get the audio from a screen recording?

Screen recordings are videos in Photos. Share one to Extract Audio and export it. If it is silent, the microphone was off while recording.

Learn more →

How can I listen to a lecture video as audio?

Convert the lecture video to MP3 and save it to Files. You can then listen offline with the screen off, using far less storage.

Learn more →

Can I make a ringtone from a video on iPhone?

Yes. Trim the audio to 30 seconds, save it to Files, then on iOS 26 tap Share → Use as Ringtone. On older iOS use GarageBand.

Learn more →
