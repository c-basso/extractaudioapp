How to Get the Audio from a Screen Recording on iPhone

Updated September 2026 · 2 min read · by Vladimir Ivakhnenko

Quick answeriPhone screen recordings are saved as videos in Photos. To get the audio, open the recording, tap Share → Extract Audio, trim if needed and tap Extract Audio in Extract Audio from Video⁺. If the file is silent, the recording itself captured no sound — turn the microphone on before recording.

Screen recordings are a common way to capture a voice message, a call on speaker, or a clip from an app. Here is how to keep just the audio.

Step by step

  1. 1.Find the recording in Photos

    Screen recordings appear in Photos → Media Types → Screen Recordings.

  2. 2.Share to Extract Audio

    Open the recording, tap Share and choose Extract Audio.

  3. 3.Trim to the part you need (optional)

    Tap Trim Video, drag the yellow handles to the start and end of the part you want, preview it, and tap Save.

  4. 4.Extract and save

    Tap Extract Audio and save the MP3 or M4A to Files.

Why is my screen recording silent?

  • Microphone off: in Control Center, long-press the Screen Recording button and tap Microphone to record your voice.
  • Silent mode: some app sounds are muted when the phone is in silent mode.
  • Protected content: many streaming apps block audio in screen recordings — that is by design and cannot be bypassed.

Respect privacy

Only record and save calls or conversations with the consent of everyone involved, and follow the recording laws where you live.

Frequently asked questions

Can I convert a screen recording to MP3?

Yes. Screen recordings are regular videos, so you can extract their audio as MP3 or M4A.

Where are screen recordings saved on iPhone?

In the Photos app, under Media Types → Screen Recordings.

Why can I not hear audio in my screen recording?

The microphone was off or the app you recorded blocks audio capture. Check that the recording plays with sound before extracting.
